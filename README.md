# Fireworksi

**Create a celebration worth remembering.**

Fireworksi is a digital celebration platform in development. It aims to bring together interactive digital fireworks, personalised shareable celebrations, creative inspiration and practical education about real-fireworks safety.

**Live website:** https://fireworksi.netlify.app/  
**Source repository:** https://github.com/Augak/Fireworksi

## Project direction

Fireworksi is being developed around four connected experiences:

- **Create:** interactive digital fireworks and personalised celebration pages.
- **Discover:** celebration ideas, examples, photography and inspiration.
- **Learn:** tutorials and evidence-based information about real fireworks, their risks and responsible safety practices.
- **Connect:** useful celebration resources, vendors and future entertainment.

## Development principles

- Mobile-first and usable on lower-powered Android devices.
- Fast loading, low-bandwidth friendly and progressively enhanced.
- Accessible keyboard navigation, semantic markup and screen-reader support.
- Real working features; no fake controls or misleading placeholders.
- Privacy-conscious handling of celebration messages and shared links.
- Respect reduced-motion preferences; sound is opt-in.
- Digital fireworks are clearly distinguished from physical fireworks.
- Physical-fireworks content is educational and safety-focused. It will not provide instructions for manufacturing, modifying or combining explosive materials.

## Technology direction

The final stack will be confirmed after the existing website source is reviewed. Current candidates include HTML, CSS and JavaScript, Three.js for selected visual effects, Supabase for shareable celebration data, and Netlify for hosting. Remotion may be considered for a later video-export feature.

These are planned options, not claims that integrations are already configured.
